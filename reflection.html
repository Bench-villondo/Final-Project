PERSONAL REFLECTION

What being a light
means to me.

Faith, kindness, and good choices—both online and offline.

For me, putting God first means choosing what is right in my everyday life. I know I am not perfect, but I try to learn from my mistakes and become a better person each day.

When I use the internet, I should also remember to be respectful and careful with my words. A simple comment can affect someone's feelings, so I want to share things that are kind, true, and helpful. I should think before I post and respect other people's opinions and privacy.

This project reminds me that technology is not only for entertainment. It can also be used to learn, help others, and spread positive messages. I may not be able to change the whole world, but I can start by making good choices. With God's guidance, I want to be a light to the people around me, both online and offline.
